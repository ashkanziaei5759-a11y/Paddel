# Lemo-Opuscar Reel (Swiss): treatment

Style: **Swiss Motion Graphics** (`styles/swiss-motion/STYLE.md`). 40 s, 1080×1920 vertical (9:16), 24 fps.
Voice: the user's second recording (Persian), cleaned and lightly edited. No TTS.
**All on-screen text is Persian**, set in **Vazirmatn** (one family, weights 300 to 900). Persian runs right to left, so "flush left, ragged right" becomes **flush right, ragged left**. Arabic script must never be letter-spaced (it breaks the joins), so tracking stays at 0.
Structure locked to the user's brief: 0–6 hook · 6–17 intro · 17–32 how it works · 32–40 end, with 0.5 s breaths between sections.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The programme with one exception (chosen).** The grid is the system. Three words are knife-cut until only a giant Persian zero «۰» is left. In Persian that zero is a dot, and it's the one orange element, the protagonist. The dot is the agent: it's the only thing that moves off-grid (linear, unsnapped). It does the work in step 3, and at the end it turns into the Claude Code logo.
2. **The specimen sheet.** 39 cells fill one by one like a type specimen, each a style. Rejected as the spine: it can't carry "how it works" or the call to action. It survives as the 39-cell grid in section 2.
3. **The timetable.** 40 rows = 40 seconds, each filling as the film plays. Rejected: too abstract for a 40-second reel. The hook needs a word on screen in the first second.

## 2. Logline and arc

*A Swiss grid cuts away everything a film supposedly needs and leaves a zero, then shows that the zero (an orange dot) is the agent that builds the film from one link and one story.*

- **Setup:** «دوربین»، «ادیتور»، «فوتیج» are knife-cut. «۰» is left.
- **Turn:** the dot sets a grid of **39 cells**, one per style («۳۹ سبک»). Five are named, and the rest ripple in.
- **Ending:** three rules; then the dot runs the machine (code, render, music) by itself. A pull-out to a wall of pages shows this film's own layouts, and they rebuild themselves. A push into one page gives the end card: a comment field with «ریپو», and the dot lands as the Claude Code logo.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Swiss rail and transit signage systems in motion (SBB-style wayfinding), and Experimental Jetset identity animations.** *Learn:* one grid that never breaks, numbers as giant typographic objects, snaps on the beat, the rhythm of long and short. *Don't take:* any logo, pictogram set, typeface or specific layout.
- **Persian modernist poster design (the 1960s–70s Tehran school: Morteza Momayez, Ghobad Shiva).** *Learn:* Persian letterforms held as strong blocks in a strict layout, bold scale contrast, a single accent colour. *Don't take:* their posters' compositions or lettering.

## 4. Grid and palette

- Frame 1080×1920. **6 columns** of 136 px with 24 px gutters and 72 px side margins. 24 px baseline. **Rows of 136 + 24 px**, so square modules. Few, wide modules: mass, not a chart.
- Composition margin: content sits between y 216 and y 1560. The top 11% and the bottom 19% stay quiet for Instagram's UI, apart from the caption row at y 1430–1560.
- Palette: page `#F2F0EA`, paper `#FFFFFF`, ink `#141414`, greys `#D9D6CE` / `#BEBAB1` (grid lines, secondary voice), **signal `#D97757`**: Claude's own orange, so the protagonist and the logo it becomes share one colour. Only the dot carries it.
- No gradients, shadows or grain (mux grain 0).

## 6. Cue map

- 120 BPM, 4/4. Snaps land on beats (0.5 s) or 8ths (0.25 s) and start one note value early, using the precise ease `cubic-bezier(.7,0,.2,1)`. Lines and the dot move linearly.
- **Music:** a motorik pulse: a soft synthesized kick and closed hat, a muted bass on 8ths, and a marimba ostinato. Everything is synthesized, quiet, and sits ≥ 12 dB under the voice.
  - Section 1: hat ticks only. Each knife cut = a guillotine snick. A hard stop, then the «۰» lands with a numeral thunk and a low marimba note.
  - Section 2: the kick enters on the title, the marimba ostinato on «۳۹». Each named cell = a block snap + a marimba note. The ripple = a fast run of tiny clacks.
  - Section 3: **one instrument added per rule**: ۱ + bass, ۲ + marimba counter-line, ۳ + the dot's sine glide (the only thing that sings) while the drums drop.
  - Section 4: a hard stop at 32.0; the wall is a dry paper-slap cascade with the music thinned. It resolves on the end card.
- Foley = materials of print, dry, no reverb: letterpress clack (snaps), ruling-pen "tss" (lines), guillotine snick (knife cuts), paper slap (wipes), numeral thunk, typewriter keys («ریپو»).

## 8. Type and subtitles

- Board type: Vazirmatn 900 for the words and numbers; 500 for labels; 300 for the tiny technical notes. Persian digits (۰–۹) throughout.
- **Subtitles are part of the layout:** the caption row at y 1430–1560, flush right on the right margin, with a 1 px rule above. Each clause appears word by word on its spoken timing (clip-reveal), bold 44 px. Hold ≥ max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s), asserted in code. Also exported as `.srt`.
- Names are transliterated so all text is Persian: «لمو اوپوس‌کار», «کلاد کد». The logo is the official Claude mark.
