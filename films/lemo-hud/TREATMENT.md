# Lemo-Opuscar Reel (Hologram HUD): treatment

Style: **Sci-fi Hologram HUD** (`styles/hologram-hud/STYLE.md`). 40 s, 1080×1920 vertical (9:16), 24 fps.
Voice: the user's second recording (Persian), cleaned and edited exactly as in the Swiss version (same cuts, same word map).
Text: **"LEMO-OPUSCAR" in English** (Rajdhani, all caps, as the style's title type), **everything else in Persian** (Vazirmatn), with Persian digits. The real images come from the project's GitHub page: the gallery mosaic (`docs/cover.jpg`) and the per-style frames (`docs/frames/*.jpg`), MIT licence.
Structure locked to the user's brief: 0–6 hook · 6–17 intro · 17–32 how it works · 32–40 end, with 0.5 s breaths.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Spec walkthrough of a film studio that isn't there (chosen).** The HUD scans for what a film needs: a camera, an editor, footage. Each target locks, and its value rolls in **red** to «۰». Then the scanner finds the real thing, the repo: a hologram browser panel with the GitHub page, and "LEMO-OPUSCAR" rolls in like a value. «۳۹» locks. Style frames fly in as holographic cards. Then three steps as an assembly sequence. Then the whole HUD ignites once into a solid hologram ("this film was made the same way"), and the CTA.
2. **Upgrade.** An old studio (camera, editor) and the new one share the pad, and every number rolls from old to new. Rejected: too slow for the hook, and the narration lists rather than compares.
3. **Loop screen.** No narrative, five specs cycling. Rejected: we have a voice with a story.

## 2. Logline and arc

*A holographic scanner looks for a camera, an editor and footage and finds zero of each; instead it finds one open-source repo that turns a link and a story into this film.*

- **Setup:** three wireframes (camera, monitor, film reel) scanned in → locked → «۰» in red, scan-erased.
- **Turn:** the GitHub page materialises as a hologram panel. LEMO-OPUSCAR rolls in. «۳۹» locks. Real style frames lock in one by one, then a carousel of the rest orbits the pad.
- **Ending:** three steps lock in order. The pad ignites (the solid hologram + beam, once). End card: comment plate «ریپو» + the Claude Code logo.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Film UI design of the 2010s (holographic tables, the *Oblivion* bubble-ship screens):** hairlines, plates, numbers that compute. *Learn:* depth layers, corner brackets, rolling values, the restraint of one hue. *Don't take:* layouts, icons, fonts, colours.
- **Apple/Nothing product teaser grammar:** name → three specs → CTA in under 40 s. *Learn:* one fact per beat, holds long enough to read. *Don't take:* any product look.

## 4. Palette (more colour, as asked)

- **Hue (lines, HUD, plates): ice-cyan `#3FE6FF`.** **Accent (locked values only): red `#FF3B4E`.** Red marks every value at the frame it becomes true: the «۰»s, «۳۹», the picked style, the ✓s, «ریپو».
- **More colour, without breaking the style's logic:** the GitHub images keep their full colour (the style's `keep` exception, here for the project's own imagery) behind a holographic interlace, and the Claude Code logo keeps its orange `#D97757` on the end card. A violet `#9B7BFF` secondary line level gives the background grid and far depth a second cold tone.
- Stage: near-black blue `#04070C`, radial lift, dot grid, vignette.

## 6. Sound

Synth palette per the style: a saw arpeggio with ping-pong delay (scooped 1–4 kHz for the voice), an 8th bass, synth hats and claps, detuned saw pads, at 120 BPM. Foley: locks = thump + clack + two-tone beep in key; rolls tick at frame rate; a projector hum (40/80/120 Hz). One beat of true silence at 32.0; a drop (glide + wide chord) on the solid hologram. Music and foley are ducked with a held envelope under the voice. −14 LUFS.

## 7. Type and subtitles

Subtitles are HUD: a dark plate with corner brackets and voiceprint bars moving with the speech, bottom centre, Vazirmatn Medium, near-white. Hold ≥ max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s). The name and the micro data are Latin (Rajdhani / Share Tech Mono); every word a viewer reads is Persian, except the name.
